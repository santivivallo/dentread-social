ADA to launch CBCT training for dentists, teams

2026-10-02 · DentRead

  • The American Dental Association presented a new training on cone beam computed tomography that starts on November 6. The program offers two modalities with continuing education credits, a basic one for dentists and clinic staff, and an advanced one exclusively for professionals that includes case discussion and a virtual study group. Both options will be led by specialist Trishul Allareddy and will allow access to recorded content after the live broadcast.
