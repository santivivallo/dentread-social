Proposed ADA standard specifies requirements for comprehensive, consistent dental EOBs

2026-08-14 · DentRead

  • The American Dental Association presented a proposal to standardize explanation of benefits reports across clinics and insurers, with a deadline to submit comments until August 21. This measure seeks to unify shared data to clarify the financial obligations of treatments and streamline claims. If approved, practices will have a lower administrative follow up burden and greater predictability in their collections.
