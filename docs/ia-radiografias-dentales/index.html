AI applied to dental radiographs

2026-08-12 · DentRead

DentRead applies artificial intelligence to the analysis of dental radiographic images.

The system processes radiographs and organizes the information for the professional to work with.

A radiograph holds information that often goes underused during the appointment.

Computational support can add a systematic second look at the image.
