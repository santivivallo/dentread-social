US dental market data, with sources

35 verified figures · updated 2026-10-30

Every figure was read in its primary source before publication, with publication, page and date. None is a DentRead result.

Acceso

  • How many US adults lack dental coverage?

    About 72 million US adults lack dental coverage.

    CareQuest Institute, Out-of-Pocket Costs for Dental Care (p. 1, 2025-11)

  • Are more US adults uninsured for dental care than for medical care?

    27% of US adults have no dental insurance. For medical insurance the figure is 9.5%, about 26 million adults.

    CareQuest Institute, Out-of-Pocket Costs for Dental Care (p. 1, 2025-11)

  • How many US adults who plan to see a dentist actually go?

    77% of adults say they plan to visit the dentist within the next year. 37% actually visited within the last year.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, US Oral Health and Well-Being (p. 6, 2024-01)

  • Does dental insurance change how often people visit the dentist?

    58% of those with private dental insurance visited the dentist in the past year, compared to 28% of those with no insurance.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, National Trends in Dental Care Use, Coverage and Cost Barriers (p. 4, 2026-04)

  • How does income affect dental visits in the US?

    58% of the population at or above 400% of the federal poverty level visited the dentist, compared to 28% of the population below 100% FPL.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, National Trends in Dental Care Use, Coverage and Cost Barriers (p. 4, 2026-04)

  • Do children or working-age adults visit the dentist more in the US?

    53% of children visited the dentist in the past year, compared to 41% of working-age adults.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, National Trends in Dental Care Use, Coverage and Cost Barriers (p. 4, 2026-04)

  • Are there ethnic gaps in dental visits in the US?

    53% of the white population visited the dentist in the past year, compared to 32% of the Hispanic population.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, National Trends in Dental Care Use, Coverage and Cost Barriers (p. 4, 2026-04)

  • How many US states offer extensive adult dental benefits in Medicaid?

    As of 2025, 38 states and DC offer enhanced dental benefits for adult Medicaid beneficiaries.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, Dental Care in Medicaid Programs (p. 3, 2025-12)

  • How does Medicaid dental reimbursement compare with dentist fees and private insurance?

    Medicaid reimbursement in most states falls far below 50% of dentist charges and 60% of private insurance reimbursement.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, Dental Care in Medicaid Programs (p. 4, 2025-12)

  • Do DSO-affiliated dentists still accept Medicaid?

    As of 2024, 49% of dentists affiliated with DSOs participate in Medicaid/CHIP, down from 67% in 2015.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, Dental Care in Medicaid Programs (p. 13, 2025-12)

  • Do female or male dentists participate more in Medicaid?

    As of 2024, 45% of female dentists participate in Medicaid/CHIP compared to 39% of male dentists.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, Dental Care in Medicaid Programs (p. 12, 2025-12)

  • Do Medicaid recipients also have private dental insurance?

    About 5% of Medicaid recipients also have private dental insurance.

    CareQuest Institute, Out-of-Pocket Costs for Dental Care (p. 6, 2025-11)

  • Do children on Medicaid or CHIP visit the dentist?

    In 27 states, fewer than half of child Medicaid/CHIP beneficiaries visited a dentist in the past year.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, Dental Care in Medicaid Programs (p. 23, 2025-12)

  • How many US adults have lost their dental coverage?

    About 8.3 million adults reported losing their dental coverage.

    CareQuest Institute, Out-of-Pocket Costs for Dental Care (p. 3, 2025-11)

  • How many older US adults have untreated cavities?

    20.2% of US adults aged 65 and older have at least one untreated cavity.

    CDC/NCHS, FastStats Oral and Dental Health (revisado 2026-01-16), Health, United States 2019, tabla 28 (NHANES 2015-2018)

  • How many US adults see a dentist each year?

    65.5% of US adults aged 18 and older had a dental exam or cleaning in the past year.

    CDC/NCHS, FastStats Oral and Dental Health (revisado 2026-01-16), NHIS 2023 Early Release

  • Do US children visit the dentist more than adults?

    86.9% of US children aged 2-17 had a dental visit in the past year, compared with 65.5% of adults.

    CDC/NCHS, FastStats Oral and Dental Health (revisado 2026-01-16), Health, United States 2019, tabla DentCh (niños, 2019) y NHIS 2023 (adultos)

  • How many older US adults have lost all their teeth?

    13.2% of US adults aged 65 and older have lost all their natural teeth.

    CDC/NCHS, FastStats Oral and Dental Health (revisado 2026-01-16), NHANES 2017-marzo 2020, NHSR 158 tablas 4 y 9

  • How common is complete tooth loss worldwide?

    WHO estimates global complete tooth loss prevalence at 23% among people aged 60 and older, and almost 7% among those aged 20 and older.

    OMS, hoja informativa Salud bucodental (17-03-2025), a partir de GBD 2021

Clinica

  • How common are cavities in US children and adolescents?

    46.0% of US children and adolescents aged 2-19 have treated or untreated caries in at least one tooth.

    CDC/NCHS, FastStats Oral and Dental Health (revisado 2026-01-16), NHANES 2017-marzo 2020, NHSR 158 tablas 4 y 9

  • How many young US adults have untreated cavities?

    25.9% of US adults aged 20-44 have at least one untreated cavity.

    CDC/NCHS, FastStats Oral and Dental Health (revisado 2026-01-16), Health, United States 2019, tabla 28 (NHANES 2015-2018)

  • How common is severe periodontal disease worldwide?

    WHO estimates more than one billion cases of severe periodontal disease worldwide.

    OMS, hoja informativa Salud bucodental (17-03-2025), a partir de GBD 2021

  • How many people do oral diseases affect worldwide?

    WHO estimates oral diseases affect nearly 3.7 billion people, and untreated caries in permanent teeth is the most common health condition worldwide.

    OMS, hoja informativa Salud bucodental (17-03-2025), a partir de GBD 2021

Mercado

  • Are fewer US dentists owning their practices?

    73% of dentists were practice owners in 2023, compared to 85% in 2005.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, US Dentist Workforce 2025 (p. 27, 2025-08)

  • How many US dentists work in group practices?

    In 2024, about 2 in 3 dentists practiced in a group setting; only 34% practiced in a single location with no other dentists.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, US Dentist Workforce 2025 (p. 23, 2025-08)

  • How common is DSO affiliation among early-career dentists?

    In several states, more than 1 in 4 dentists up to 10 years out of dental school are affiliated with a DSO.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, US Dentist Workforce 2025 (p. 37, 2025-08)

  • How many US dentists are recruiting dental hygienists?

    In Q4 2025, 31.4% of dentists reported recruiting dental hygienists — about 1 in 3.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, State of the US Dental Economy Q4 2025 (p. 21, 2025-12)

  • What changed in CDT 2026?

    CDT 2026 contains 60 code changes effective January 1, 2026: 31 additions, 14 revisions, 6 deletions and 9 editorial changes.

    ADA News, New CDT codes you should know for 2026 (2025-09-29)

  • Is there a CDT code for artificial intelligence in dentistry?

    None of the additions the ADA highlighted in CDT 2026 relate to artificial intelligence, and no dedicated CDT code for AI has been reported.

    ADA News, New CDT codes you should know for 2026 (2025-09-29)

  • Do the new CDT 2026 codes create new dental procedures?

    The highlighted CDT 2026 additions — saliva testing, cracked tooth testing, duplicate dentures, peri-implantitis debridement and occlusal guard cleaning — document services practices already performed. The code does not create the procedure; it makes it billable.

    ADA News, New CDT codes you should know for 2026 (2025-09-29)

  • How many dentists report being overworked?

    32.4% of surveyed dentists report being overworked.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, HPI Perspectives (julio 2026), panel trimestral de dentistas de EE.UU.

Tecnologia

  • How many dentists use artificial intelligence?

    43.3% of surveyed dentists use AI for at least one task in their practice, and another 26.4% say they plan to.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, HPI Perspectives (julio 2026), panel trimestral de dentistas de EE.UU.

  • How many dentists use AI for dental imaging and diagnostics?

    22.8% of surveyed dentists use AI for imaging and diagnostics, and another 25.4% who do not use it today plan to use it for that.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, HPI Perspectives (julio 2026), panel trimestral de dentistas de EE.UU.

  • Do dentists use AI to explain findings to patients?

    13.2% of surveyed dentists use AI to explain clinical findings to their patients, while 68.3% say they do not plan to use it for that.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, HPI Perspectives (julio 2026), panel trimestral de dentistas de EE.UU.

  • Do dentists use AI for treatment recommendations?

    82.6% of surveyed dentists do not plan to use AI for treatment recommendations, and fewer than 5% use it for that today.

    ADA Health Policy Institute, HPI Perspectives (julio 2026), panel trimestral de dentistas de EE.UU.
