ADA urges CMS to account for dental data in Medicaid IT standards

By Santiago Vivallo, DDS · CEO, DentRead

2026-09-28 · DentRead

  • The American Dental Association asked the US government to include dental data formats in the new computer rules for the Medicaid system. This measure seeks to ensure that clinical records and images can be shared correctly between different providers without losing specific oral area information. If implemented, the update will facilitate the exchange of patient files between health professionals and will avoid the useless repetition of X-rays.
