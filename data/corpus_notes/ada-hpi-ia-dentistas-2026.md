# ADA HPI — "Dentists Use AI to Make Appointments More Efficient, but Draw the Line at Clinical Decision Making"

Nota de corpus verificada contra la fuente primaria el 2026-09-22, leyendo la

https://www.ada.org/resources/research/health-policy-institute/dental-practice-research/dentists-ai-usage-and-attitudes
Base: panel trimestral de dentistas de EE.UU. ("large national survey").

## Citas textuales

"more than two out of five responding dentists (43.3%) are using AI for at
least one type of task within their dental practices, and another one-quarter
(26.4%) say they are planning to do so in the future."

"Around one-fifth of responding dentists (22.8%) reported using AI for imaging
and diagnostics. More than one in ten use AI to explain clinical findings to
their patients (13.2%) and for insurance verification (13.6%). Around one in
ten dentists use AI for business analytics (10.1%), social media (10.7%), and
reception or front desk check-in (10.1%). But when it comes to clinical
decision making, AI has a minimal presence. Less than 5% of dentists report
currently using AI for patient treatment recommendations."

"One-quarter (25.4%) of dentists are not using AI today but plan to use it for
imaging and diagnostics in the future and around one-third plan to use it for
charting and note taking (34.8%) and insurance verification (32.6%)."

"four out of five (82.6%) do not plan to use AI for treatment recommendations
and two-thirds (68.3%) do not plan to use it to explain clinical findings to
patients."

"Around one-third (32.4%) of dentists report being overworked."

## Lo que esta nota NO autoriza a decir

Adopcion no es respaldo: que el 22,8% use IA para imagenologia no dice nada
sobre si funciona, ni sobre precision diagnostica. Esta fuente NO contiene
ninguna metrica de desempeño y no se puede usar para hablar de exactitud.

La nota incluye citas de dentistas, entre ellas "I've tried it with x-ray
reading and it was about 30% accurate". Es una percepcion individual recogida
en una encuesta, NO una medicion. No usarla como cifra.

El 43,3% es "al menos una tarea", y la mayoria de esas tareas son
administrativas. Presentarlo como "43% usa IA clinica" seria falso.

Y la incomoda: el 68,3% dice que NO piensa usar IA para explicar hallazgos a
los pacientes. Es evidencia en contra del modulo de explicacion al paciente de
DentRead y hay que tratarla como tal, no esconderla.
