# OMS — hoja informativa "Oral health"

Nota de corpus verificada contra la fuente primaria el 2026-09-22, leyendo la
página completa. Fuente: World Health Organization, fact sheet "Oral health",
fechada 17 March 2025, https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/oral-health
Las estimaciones provienen del Global Burden of Disease 2021.

## Citas textuales

"It is estimated that oral diseases affect nearly 3.7 billion people."

"Untreated dental caries (tooth decay) in permanent teeth is the most common
health condition according to the Global Burden of Disease 2021."

"Severe periodontal diseases are estimated to affect more than 1 billion
cases worldwide."

"The estimated global average prevalence of complete tooth loss is almost 7%
among people aged 20 years or older. For people aged 60 years or older, a much
higher global prevalence of 23% has been estimated."

"Latest estimates show that 1 billion people are affected [by oro-dental
trauma], with a prevalence of around 20% for children up to 12 years old."

"Paying for necessary oral health care is among the leading reasons for
catastrophic health expenditures."

## Cifras como se usan en facts.json

Enfermedades bucodentales en el mundo: 3.700 millones de personas (3.7 billion).
Enfermedad periodontal grave: mas de 1.000 millones de casos (1 billion).
Perdida total de dientes: 23% en 60 años o mas, casi 7% en 20 años o mas.

## Lo que esta nota NO autoriza a decir

Son estimaciones globales de carga de enfermedad, no prevalencias de EE.UU.
No mezclar con las cifras de CDC/NCHS en la misma frase sin decir que una es
mundial y la otra nacional. La OMS no publica acá nada sobre radiografías,
imagenología ni IA: no usar esta fuente para esos temas.
